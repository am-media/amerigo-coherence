# Architectural change
**When:** A concrete requirement cannot be met reasonably within existing boundaries.
**Decide:** Document the failed constraint, minimal alternatives, migration risk and rollback. Obtain explicit approval of boundary changes.
**Build:** Change the smallest necessary interfaces/modules. No speculative general-purpose platforms.
**Verify:** Architecture constraints, migrations, compatibility and real acceptance scenarios.
**Stop:** The approved requirement is satisfied, with decisions recorded. No continuous redesign.
